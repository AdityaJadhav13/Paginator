      Checks

        Drag a story to move it; drag its right or bottom edge, or its corner, to resize. The left and right arrows move the selected story by a column, up and down by half a millimetre (with Shift, 5 mm); Delete takes it off the page. Double-click a story, or press Enter, to type its text: the page is set again when you pause. Every change is set by the same engine that makes the print file, and kept as you go; Save page makes the page's PDF and checks it.

        Shift+L locks the selected story in place for this layout, or unlocks it: a locked story keeps its place, width and height and stays on the page, and Try another layout lays the other stories out around it. A right-click on a story, or Shift+F10, opens its menu; on the page's empty space it unlocks every story.

        A (or Crop photo) moves the selected story's photo in its frame: drag it, or use the arrow keys; Enlarge enlarges it as far as it still prints sharp. Double-click the photo, or press Set focal point, and click its subject: the frame keeps it as near its centre as it can when the story is resized or laid out again. V or Esc stops; Reset crop prints the photo as the layout crops it.

        Shift+2 zooms to the selected story, Shift+1 shows the whole page and Shift+3 its width; Shift+R shows or hides the rulers (millimetres from the page's top-left corner, with the columns numbered).
